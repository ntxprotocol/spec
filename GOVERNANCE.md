# Governance

The NTX Working Group follows the
[Community Specification Governance Policy 1.0](community-specification/05-governance.md):
decisions by consensus, written reasons, and an open appeal process.

## Roles

| Role | Who |
| --- | --- |
| Maintainer | Temitayo ([CamelCase Technologies Ltd](https://ntxprotocol.com)) |
| Editor | Temitayo |
| Participants | Everyone who has contributed under the Community Specification License |

New Maintainers and Editors are added with the approval of the Participants. We want more than one
organisation in these roles as soon as other serious implementers join.

## Stewardship

NTX was started by CamelCase Technologies Ltd. The goal is a neutral standard, not a product
feature. Once other organisations ship NTX in their products, we intend to move the specification
to a neutral foundation, chosen together with those implementers.

## Status of documents

Following the governance policy, a specification moves through these stages:

1. **Pre-Draft** — proposed text, open for discussion. *(NTX 0.1 is here today.)*
2. **Draft** — approved as the basis for further work.
3. **Approved** — the Working Group agrees it meets its scope.

## Trademarks

"NTX" and "NTX compatible" may only be used for products that pass the NTX conformance suite.
The full trademark policy will be published before the first release.
